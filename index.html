hexis

a quiet habit journal for iPhone and Apple Watch

hexis is a minimal, ink-on-paper habit tracker. one tap a day, one hexagon filled, streaks that forgive a rest day. a year of your practice on a single page.

hexis was called tally until august 2026 — same app, same developer. this site kept its old address through the change.

support

found a bug, have a question, or want to suggest something? email martyelenjikkal@gmail.com and you'll hear back from the developer directly. that address is also where a report from a group arrives, and those are read within a day.

subscriptions

hexis+ is billed through your App Store account. to change or cancel a plan, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone. purchases can be restored on any device signed in to the same Apple ID, from settings inside the app. hexis+ is sold as a yearly or a weekly plan; the monthly plan and the one-time lifetime unlock are no longer sold, but are still honoured if you hold one. the full billing terms — renewal, any free trial, and switching plans — are in the terms of use.

privacy

your journal never leaves your device unless you send it — no analytics, no tracking, no hexis server. the one thing that does leave is a check on whether you have bought hexis+, which is handled by RevenueCat and is told nothing about your habits. the full policy is here: privacy policy.
