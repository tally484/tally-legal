hexis

a quiet habit journal for iPhone and Apple Watch

hexis is a minimal, ink-on-paper habit tracker. one tap a day, one hexagon filled, streaks that forgive a rest day. a year of your practice on a single page.

hexis was called tally until august 2026 — same app, same developer. this site kept its old address through the change.

support

found a bug, have a question, or want to suggest something? email martyelenjikkal@gmail.com to reach the developer directly.

subscriptions

hexis+ is billed through your App Store account. to change or cancel a plan, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone. purchases can be restored on any device signed in to the same Apple ID, from settings inside the app. hexis+ is sold as a yearly or a weekly plan; the monthly plan and the one-time lifetime unlock are no longer sold, but are still honoured if you hold one. the full billing terms — renewal, the free trial on the yearly plan, switching plans, and how a price change is handled — are in the terms of use.

privacy

your journal is stored on your devices, with optional private iCloud sync for habits and related journal state. routines stay local. the app also sends a habit snapshot to your paired Apple Watch. there are no ads, tracking or developer-operated journal servers; groups are hosted in the group creator’s own iCloud. Apple handles purchases; information you deliberately email to support is received by the developer. see the privacy policy for the full details, including backups and signatures.

groups

a group lets a few people keep one habit side by side. it needs hexis+ for each person and an iCloud account, and people join by private invitation — there is no public link, no feed and nothing to browse. a group is hosted in its creator's own iCloud, so the developer can't read it. every member row offers report and block; blocking is instant and local to your device, and we aim to answer reports within 24 hours. what a group shares is listed in the privacy policy, and the conduct rules are in the terms.

groups made in builds before this release could use a public invitation link. installing the new version doesn't change those — open the group and check Manage Access, or end it. see the note on earlier groups.
