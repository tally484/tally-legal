Watch it all add up.

every day you showed up, kept in ink

Every mark stays on the page. Look back at streaks, totals and consistency in a record you can read at a glance. Track a simple yes or no, or set an amount, like 20 pages or 30 minutes, and check in as you go.

Miss a day. Keep going.

mends, not guilt

Your journal starts with three mends, and every full week you keep a habit earns another, up to three at a time. Use one to carry a streak over a missed day, without pretending the day never happened. Then step back and see the whole year, one hexagon for every day you showed up.

Private by design.

your journal is yours

There's no hexis account to make and no server holding your journal. It lives on your devices, and only in your own iCloud if you turn on sync.

No ads, no tracking
Nothing in hexis follows you around the web, and nothing is sold.
Your iCloud, not ours
Optional sync keeps habits and their progress in your private iCloud. Routines stay on your device.
Groups you invite to
A group shares one habit with people you invite yourself. It has no public feed and no profiles anyone can discover.
