hexis

a quiet habit journal for iPhone and Apple Watch

hexis is a minimal, ink-on-paper habit tracker. one tap a day, one hexagon filled, streaks that forgive a rest day. a year of your practice on a single page.

hexis was called tally until august 2026 — same app, same developer. this site kept its old address through the change.

support

found a bug, have a question, or want to suggest something? email martyelenjikkal@gmail.com to reach the developer directly. group reports go to the same address; the aim is to respond within 24 hours.

subscriptions

hexis+ is billed through your App Store account. to change or cancel a plan, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone. purchases can be restored on any device signed in to the same Apple ID, from settings inside the app. hexis+ is sold as a yearly or a weekly plan; the monthly plan and the one-time lifetime unlock are no longer sold, but are still honoured if you hold one. the full billing terms — renewal, the free trial on the yearly plan, switching plans, and how a price change is handled — are in the terms of use.

privacy

your journal is stored on your devices, with optional private iCloud sync for habits and related journal state. routines stay local. the app also sends a habit snapshot to your paired Apple Watch, and groups share selected information through iCloud. there are no ads, tracking or developer-operated journal servers. Apple handles purchases; information you deliberately email to support is received by the developer. see the privacy policy for the full details, including backups and signatures.

groups

a group shares one habit's marks and a display name you choose, in an iCloud area hosted by whoever made the group. an invite link works for anyone who has it, so share it carefully. every member's row carries a block — immediate, and it hides that person from every group you share with them — and a report, which reaches the address above. you can leave a group at any time, and if you made it you can end it for everyone. ending a group has to be done in the app; deleting the app on its own leaves the group running for its members.
