tally

a quiet habit journal for iPhone

tally is a minimal, ink-on-paper habit tracker. one tap a day, real tally marks, streaks that forgive a rest day.

support

found a bug, have a question, or want to suggest something? email martyelenjikkal@gmail.com and you'll hear back from the developer directly.

subscriptions

tally plus is billed through your App Store account. to change or cancel a plan, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone, or use "manage subscription" inside the app. purchases can be restored on any device signed into the same Apple ID.

privacy
