Hexis+ subscriptions

Hexis+ offers yearly and weekly auto-renewing subscriptions in one subscription group. Creating and joining a group is one of its benefits, and each person in a group needs their own subscription. The app and Apple’s purchase confirmation show the benefits, billing period and price in your local currency before you subscribe.

  • Payment and renewal. Apple bills your App Store account. The yearly plan renews each year and the weekly plan each week unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Renewal is charged within the 24 hours before the period ends at the renewal price applicable to your subscription. The developer does not receive your card details.
  • Seven-day free trial. The yearly plan offers a one-week trial to eligible Apple Accounts; the weekly plan does not offer a trial. Apple determines eligibility, with one introductory offer per subscription group per Apple Account. The trial automatically becomes a paid yearly subscription at the price shown before purchase unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the trial ends. Any unused trial portion is forfeited when purchasing a subscription where Apple’s rules provide for this.
  • Manage or cancel. Open Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your device. Cancelling a paid subscription stops its next renewal, with access continuing for the paid period. Access may end immediately if you cancel during a free trial. Deleting Hexis does not cancel a subscription.
  • Switch plans. Use your App Store subscription settings. Apple determines when the change takes effect and any adjustment or credit under its subscription rules; review the confirmation before accepting.
  • Existing purchases. Previously purchased monthly subscriptions and lifetime unlocks continue to be honoured. They are not offered for new purchases. A lifetime unlock does not renew or expire; an existing monthly subscription renews monthly until cancelled.
  • Existing yearly prices. The new yearly price does not by itself change the preserved renewal rate of an existing yearly subscriber. Your App Store subscription settings show your renewal price. If you cancel, let the subscription lapse, or change plans, do not assume that the former price will be available when you return. Any future renewal-price increase must follow Apple’s applicable notice and consent rules and applicable law.
  • Restore purchases. Use Restore Purchases in Hexis while signed in to the Apple Account used for the purchase.

Refund requests for App Store purchases can be made through Apple’s refund service. This does not limit any statutory rights you have against the developer or Apple. Contact us if a paid feature is unavailable.

Your journal and privacy

Your habits, routines, marks and signatures belong to you. There is no Hexis account or developer-operated journal server. Optional private iCloud sync transfers habits and related journal state, but not routines. The paired Apple Watch receives a habit snapshot; this includes signature data even though the Watch does not display it. Exported backups include habits, routines and signatures. Protect those files and share them only intentionally.

The Privacy Policy explains local storage, Apple services, what a group shares, and information you deliberately send to support. Sync is not a substitute for a backup, and the developer cannot restore your private journal for you.

A signature or commitment recorded in Hexis is a personal reminder, not a legal contract or payment obligation. Hexis is a habit journal, not medical, psychological or professional advice, treatment or a crisis service.

Groups

Groups lets a few people keep one habit alongside each other. It is a Hexis+ benefit and each participant needs their own Hexis+ subscription and an iCloud account. A group is hosted in its creator's private iCloud: if the creator ends the group, signs out of iCloud or deletes the app, the group ends for everyone in it. What a group does and does not share is set out in the Privacy Policy.

What you write for other people. The only things you write in Hexis that another person reads are a group's name, the name of the habit it tracks, and the name you show up under. Do not use them to harass, threaten, defame or impersonate anyone, to identify a person without their agreement, or to publish sexual, hateful or otherwise objectionable material. Do not use a group to advertise or to send anyone repeated unwanted contact. Hexis refuses some names automatically, on your device, before they are published; that check is deliberately narrow, and the fact that it allows a name is not a judgement that the name is acceptable.

Reporting. Every member's row offers Report, which sends the report to the developer and does not tell the person reported. We aim to review and respond to reports within 24 hours. Reports are also kept on record, so repeated reports about the same person are visible as a pattern. If something is urgent or unlawful, contact your local authorities — Hexis is not a crisis or emergency service.

Blocking, and what it does not do. Blocking hides that person from every group you share with them, on your device, at once and without needing a connection, and you can undo it. It is a local control: it does not delete what they have written, does not remove their access to the group, and does not notify them. It is the fastest remedy available to you and does not depend on us.

What the developer can and cannot do. A group's contents live in its creator's private iCloud, which the developer cannot read, alter or delete. So we cannot remove another person's name, marks or membership on your behalf, and we will not tell you that we have. What we can do is read your report, respond, advise on the controls that do work, act on reports in how Hexis is built — including the names the app refuses — and, where conduct appears unlawful, refer the matter to Apple or the appropriate authorities. The controls that actually end someone's participation belong to the people in the group: leave, for you; Manage Access and end group, for whoever created it.

If you created a group in an earlier build. Groups in builds before this release could be shared by a public invitation link, which let anyone holding the link join. Installing this version does not change those older shares. Open such a group and use Apple's Manage Access screen to check who has access, or end it. See the Privacy Policy.

Stopping use and deleting data

You may stop using Hexis at any time. Cancel any subscription separately before uninstalling if that is what you intend. Leave any group you are in, and end any group you created, before uninstalling if you want that done — uninstalling alone does not do it, and a group you created and abandoned will stop working for the people in it. Data in iCloud and exported backups is not automatically erased by uninstalling. See the Privacy Policy for storage and deletion details.
