Hexis+ subscriptions

Hexis+ offers yearly and weekly auto-renewing subscriptions in one subscription group. Where a version of Hexis includes Groups, creating or joining a group is a Hexis+ benefit and each participant needs their own entitlement; see Groups below. The app and Apple’s purchase confirmation show the benefits, billing period and price in your local currency before you subscribe.

  • Payment and renewal. Apple bills your App Store account. The yearly plan renews each year and the weekly plan each week unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Renewal is charged within the 24 hours before the period ends at the renewal price applicable to your subscription. The developer does not receive your card details.
  • Seven-day free trial. The yearly plan offers a one-week trial to eligible Apple Accounts; the weekly plan does not offer a trial. Apple determines eligibility, with one introductory offer per subscription group per Apple Account. The trial automatically becomes a paid yearly subscription at the price shown before purchase unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the trial ends. Any unused trial portion is forfeited when purchasing a subscription where Apple’s rules provide for this.
  • Manage or cancel. Open Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your device. Cancelling a paid subscription stops its next renewal, with access continuing for the paid period. Access may end immediately if you cancel during a free trial. Deleting Hexis does not cancel a subscription.
  • Switch plans. Use your App Store subscription settings. Apple determines when the change takes effect and any adjustment or credit under its subscription rules; review the confirmation before accepting.
  • Existing purchases. Previously purchased monthly subscriptions and lifetime unlocks continue to be honoured. They are not offered for new purchases. A lifetime unlock does not renew or expire; an existing monthly subscription renews monthly until cancelled.
  • Existing yearly prices. The new yearly price does not by itself change the preserved renewal rate of an existing yearly subscriber. Your App Store subscription settings show your renewal price. If you cancel, let the subscription lapse, or change plans, do not assume that the former price will be available when you return. Any future renewal-price increase must follow Apple’s applicable notice and consent rules and applicable law.
  • Restore purchases. Use Restore Purchases in Hexis while signed in to the Apple Account used for the purchase.

Refund requests for App Store purchases can be made through Apple’s refund service. This does not limit any statutory rights you have against the developer or Apple. Contact us if a paid feature is unavailable.

Your journal and privacy

Your habits, routines, marks and signatures belong to you. There is no Hexis account or developer-operated journal server. Optional private iCloud sync transfers habits and related journal state, but not routines. The paired Apple Watch receives a habit snapshot; this includes signature data even though the Watch does not display it. Exported backups include habits, routines and signatures. Protect those files and share them only intentionally.

The Privacy Policy explains local storage, Apple services, what a group shares, and information you deliberately send to support. Sync is not a substitute for a backup, and the developer cannot restore your private journal for you.

A signature or commitment recorded in Hexis is a personal reminder, not a legal contract or payment obligation. Hexis is a habit journal, not medical, psychological or professional advice, treatment or a crisis service.

Groups

Groups is not part of every version of Hexis. The version published on the App Store in September 2026 (1.3, build 39) does not include it, and this section applies only to a version that does. Where it is available, the feature lets a few people keep one habit alongside each other. Creating a group and joining one each require a Hexis+ entitlement and an iCloud account, for every participant. A group is hosted in its creator’s private iCloud as a shared record zone. The creator can end it explicitly; signing out or deleting the app does not by itself delete the shared CloudKit zone or revoke invitations. What a group does and does not share is set out in the Privacy Policy.

Invitations, and who can join. A group is shared through Apple’s own sharing controls, to people you add individually. Public link access is not enabled for a new group, so holding a forwarded invitation does not by itself let an uninvited Apple Account join. An invitation does not expire on a timer. Someone you invite keeps access until the group’s creator removes them through Apple’s Manage Access screen or ends the group. Everyone in a group can read it and add to it, and the invitation is the access control — invite only people you are willing to give that to. Hexis writes only your own marks, and nothing on this page promises that another participant’s device technically cannot alter what is in the shared group.

What you write for other people. The only things you write in Hexis that another person reads are a group’s name, the name of the habit it tracks, and the name you show up under. Do not use them to harass, threaten, defame or impersonate anyone, to identify a person without their agreement, or to publish sexual, hateful or otherwise objectionable material. Do not use a group to advertise or to send anyone repeated unwanted contact. Hexis refuses some names automatically, on your device, before they are published; that check is deliberately narrow, and the fact that it allows a name is not a judgement that the name is acceptable.

Reporting. Every member’s row offers Report. A report goes to the developer separately from the group: it is not written into the group, and the person reported is not notified by the app. The app tells you what actually happened — uploaded and confirmed by iCloud, saved on your device to retry later, or neither uploaded nor saved, in which case it offers an email draft or a copy you can send yourself. Reports waiting to send are listed on the Groups tab, where you can retry, email or copy them. Each report carries a reference code; quote it if you follow up by email. Hexis is made by one person, so reports are read by that person rather than by a team on a rota: every report is read, and acted on as soon as we reasonably can. We would rather say that than name a deadline we might miss. The app’s upload receipt means iCloud accepted the record; it does not by itself mean the report has been read yet. Blocking takes effect immediately and does not wait for a report — use it if you need to stop seeing someone now. If a matter is urgent or unlawful, contact your local authorities; Hexis is not a crisis or emergency service.

Blocking, and what it does not do. Blocking hides that person from every group you share with them, on your device, at once and without needing a connection, and you can undo it from the same member row. It is a local control: it does not delete what they have written, does not remove their access to the group, and does not notify them. It is the fastest remedy available to you and does not depend on us.

What the developer can and cannot do. A group’s contents live in its creator’s private iCloud, which the developer cannot read, alter or delete. So we cannot remove another person’s name, marks or membership on your behalf, and we will not tell you that we have. What we can do is read your report, respond, advise on the controls that do work, act on reports in how Hexis is built — including the names the app refuses — and, where conduct appears unlawful, refer the matter to Apple or the appropriate authorities. The controls that actually end someone’s participation belong to the people in the group: leave, for you; Manage Access and end group, for whoever created it.

If you created a group in an earlier TestFlight build. Such groups could be shared by a public invitation link, which let anyone holding the link join. Installing a later version does not convert those older shares. Where Hexis detects that a group’s share still allows link access, its invite page shows a warning and will not send another invitation from that screen; that check does not revoke a link that has already been passed on. Open the group and use Apple’s Manage Access screen to change sharing to invited people only, to check who has access, or to end the group. See the Privacy Policy.

Stopping use and deleting data

You may stop using Hexis at any time. Cancel any subscription separately before uninstalling if that is what you intend. In a Groups-enabled version, leave any group you are in and explicitly end any group you created before uninstalling if you want that done. Uninstalling alone does not delete a shared group or revoke invitations: an abandoned group stays in its creator’s iCloud and remains visible to its members, but stops being kept up to date. Ending it is the only thing that removes it for everyone. Data in iCloud and exported backups is not automatically erased by uninstalling. See the Privacy Policy for storage and deletion details.
