Privacy Policy

Hexis · Updated 12 September 2026

This policy describes version 1.3 build 39 and later, being prepared for release. Groups have been removed from that build. Earlier installed versions may still contain Groups; their data handling is described in the earlier policy and the legacy section below. The app was previously called Tally.

Overview

Hexis is a private habit journal developed by Martin Elenjikkal. There is no Hexis account or developer-operated journal server. No advertising, tracking or remote analytics SDK is included. The app does not automatically send your journal to the developer. Information can move through optional private iCloud sync, your paired Apple Watch, exports and sharing you initiate, and emails you choose to send.

Your journal and private iCloud

Habit names, icons, targets, amounts, cadence, reminders, daily marks, streaks, routines, settings, signatures and commitments are stored on your devices. Optional iCloud sync is off by default. It mirrors habits, signatures, earned-mend balance and change/deletion records to your private iCloud using Apple's key-value store. Routines are not included. Turning sync off stops further mirroring; the developer cannot read or restore your private iCloud data.

Removing the app does not erase existing iCloud data or exported backups. Manage iCloud storage through your Apple Account settings, and contact Apple Support if you need help with retained Apple-hosted data. Keep a backup before deleting storage you may need.

Apple Watch, widgets and reminders

The paired Watch receives a habit snapshot over Apple's device-to-device connection and sends habit marks back. The current snapshot includes signature data and other habit fields that the Watch does not display. Routines are not sent to the Watch. Widgets and complications use on-device journal storage; marks made on these surfaces can subsequently sync to your own devices.

Habit and routine reminders are local notifications. Routine Live Activities are driven by local state. Build 39 removes group-related remote notification registration, background handling and CloudKit sharing capabilities. No developer push server is used.

Backups and sharing

Exported backups contain habits, daily marks, amounts, routines, mend balance, signatures and commitments. Protect these files. Sending a backup or screenshot to someone gives that recipient its contents. Progress cards are generated locally and handed to the app or destination you choose, whose privacy rules then apply. There is no in-app social feed or group-sharing feature in build 39.

Legacy Groups and retained information

Removing Groups from the new app does not automatically delete old shared iCloud records, revoke old invitations or disable older installed copies. Build 39 does not load, display, join, create, update or publish social groups. Existing local group caches are left untouched, not reused by the new app.

Earlier builds could share group and selected-habit names/icons/cadence, display names/colours, day states, edit timestamps, time zones, membership identifiers and sync records in the creator's CloudKit zone. Anyone with an invite link could join. Other habits, routines and signatures were not shared with members. See the earlier policy for details.

If you still have a working older version, its Leave Group or End Group control can remove your member record or, for the creator, end the shared group. Do this before upgrading if you want that removal. Do not assume reinstalling the latest app restores those controls. The developer cannot directly erase another person's private CloudKit zone. Contact us or Apple Support for help with legacy data; we will not claim it has been deleted without confirmation.

Purchases

Apple processes purchases, trials, subscriptions and refunds through your App Store account. Hexis uses StoreKit to load products and verify entitlements. Purchases and restores communicate with Apple; valid local entitlement records can support offline access. The app does not send journal contents with purchase checks, and the developer does not receive card details. Removing a feature or uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription; manage it in your Apple Account subscription settings.

Local diagnostics

A small first-run event log is stored on your device, not transmitted. It records events such as onboarding and paywall presentation, with timestamps, but no habit names or daily marks. It is not connected to a remote analytics service.

Support, suggestions and earlier reports

Support and suggestion controls prepare emails for you to review and send from your own mail app. Opening or discarding a draft does not send it. We receive your email address, displayed sender name, message and any attachments you include. In-app messages may include the app version; suggestions also include their selected category and preparation time. They do not automatically attach your journal.

Reports from older builds may include group/member identifiers and names, the reporter's app-scoped identifier, reason, note, app version and preparation time. We use correspondence for support, resolving reported problems and safety, not advertising or marketing. It is held in the developer's email account for as long as needed for those purposes. Contact us to request access or deletion; limited records may need to be retained for legal or unresolved safety obligations.

Children

Hexis is not directed at children and does not knowingly solicit their personal information. If a child sends personal information to support, a parent or guardian can contact us to request deletion.

Changes and contact

This revision documents the removal of Groups in build 39 without claiming historical shared data has disappeared. Future material changes will be reflected here.
