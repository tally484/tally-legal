privacy policy

tally — habit journal · effective 3 july 2026

tally is built to be private. the short version: your data stays with you, and we collect nothing.

what we collect

nothing. tally has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. we never see your habits, your notes, or anything about how you use the app.

where your data lives

your habits, streaks, and reflections are stored locally on your device. deleting the app deletes them. if a future version of tally offers iCloud sync and you enable it, your data will also be stored in your private iCloud database, encrypted in transit and accessible only to your Apple ID — tally has no server and no way to read it.

purchases

subscriptions and one-time purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. we never see your payment details. you can manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store account settings.

notifications

habit reminders are local notifications, scheduled and delivered on your device. they never touch a server.

children

tally does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children — because it collects no personal information at all.

changes

if this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. since we collect nothing, changes would only ever describe new on-device or iCloud features.
